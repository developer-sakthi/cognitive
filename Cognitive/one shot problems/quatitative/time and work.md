## TIME AND WORK :

1. Three persons A, B, and C finished a piece of work. A worked for 5 days, B for 7 days, and C for 9 days. Their daily wages were in the proportion 4:3:2, and the total earnings amounted to Rs. 118. Find the daily wages of each person.

> **ans**:<br/>
> A = Rs. 8 per day<br/>
> B = Rs. 6 per day<br/>
> C = Rs. 4 per day

<br/>

2.  20 men can complete a piece of work in 10 days but after every 4 days 5 men are called off. In what time will the work be finished?
