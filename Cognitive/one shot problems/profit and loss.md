1. Rakesh and Suresh invested Rs. 30,000 and Rs. 50,000 respectively to start a business.
The ratio of the time for which Suresh and Rakesh invested their money is 3 : 4.
At the end of the year:
- Rakesh was paid Rs. 18,000 as an incentive for managing the business.
- Suresh received Rs. 40,000 from the remaining profit.<br/>
Find the total profit obtained by the business.

<p align=center><b>ans : </b> 90000</p>


<br/>
<br/>

2. A fruit seller purchased 50 kg of mangoes at the rate of Rs. 25 per kg. <br/>
    He sold some of the mangoes at the rate of Rs. 40 per kg and the remaining mangoes at the rate of Rs. 15 per kg.<br/>
    If he made an overall profit of 20%, find the quantity of mangoes sold at Rs. 15 per kg.

<p align=center><b>ans : </b> 20kg</p>

3. A retailer purchased 25 identical toys for a price Rs. P and sold some of them for Rs.P. If he calculated his profit as 8%, with selling price as a base instead of cost price then how many did he sell?

<p align=center><b>ans : </b> 23</p>
